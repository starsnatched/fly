🛞 FLYBRAIN ROVER

A skid-steer rover driven by a circuit of real neurons extracted from the MaleCNS male fruit-fly connectome (Janelia/Google, Cell 2026 — CC-BY).
One forward camera → elementary motion detectors → 165,122 spiking neurons (leaky integrate-and-fire) wired by 25.5M real connectome synapses → throttle + steer.
Explore mode: open desert, no objectives, no restarts — bumps just bounce. Add ?course=gaps to the URL for the gap-crossing trial: pits punish a fall (−1, reset), a clean crossing rewards (+0.6).
